About

Neloy Enterprise is a manufacturer, not a marketplace

Neloy Enterprise is a Bangladesh-based manufacturing and custom-design company focused on practical products for modern homes, shops and offices.

We combine aluminium, glass, mirror and board materials to create products that fit the customer's space, requirements and budget — built in our own workshop in Netrakona, for customers across Netrakona and nearby areas.
